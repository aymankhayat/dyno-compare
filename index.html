About this project

I’m Ayman Khayat, a mechanical engineering student. During an internship at a Toyota dealership I worked on hybrid powertrain diagnostics, which is where the hybrid write-up on this page comes from: regenerative braking, e-CVT behavior and battery management are what you see from the service bay, not from the brochure.

Spec sheets tell you what a car makes. They rarely tell you why two cars with similar numbers drive so differently. I built this to put the numbers next to the reasons.

Ayman on LinkedIn Source code on GitHub

What it demonstrates

  • Data integrity: every figure links to the manufacturer document it came from, and the lineup only includes cars whose makers publish every figure shown.
  • A 3D showroom built from code with three.js: each car modeled by hand from its own silhouette and sized from its published dimensions, with its signature details (the 911’s round headlights and light bar, the M3’s kidney grille, the GR Corolla’s three exhausts, the IONIQ 5 N’s pixel lights), plus orbit controls and spec hotspots.
  • Explanations generated from the cars being compared, so they quote this comparison’s own numbers.
  • Unit conversions checked by automated tests against reference values (60 mph = 96.6 km/h, 300 hp = 224 kW).
  • Shareable state: the address bar always links to the exact comparison on screen.

Specs last checked . US-market models; figures are the manufacturers’ own, stored in US units and converted to metric in the browser. Each 3D model is hand-traced from the car’s silhouette and sized from the maker’s published length, width, height and wheelbase, so the cars are to scale with one another. They’re close approximations, not official models.
