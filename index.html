About this project

I’m Ayman Khayat, a mechanical engineering student. During an internship at a Toyota dealership I worked on hybrid powertrain diagnostics, which is where the hybrid write-up on this page comes from: regenerative braking, e-CVT behavior and battery management are what you see from the service bay, not from the brochure.

Spec sheets tell you what a car makes. They rarely tell you why two cars with similar numbers drive so differently. I built this to put the numbers next to the reasons.

What it demonstrates

  • Data integrity: each figure links to the manufacturer document it came from. Where a maker doesn’t publish a figure, the page says “unverified” instead of estimating one.
  • Explanations generated from the cars being compared, so they quote this comparison’s own numbers.
  • Unit conversions checked by automated tests against reference values (60 mph = 96.6 km/h, 300 hp = 224 kW).
  • Shareable state: the address bar always links to the exact comparison on screen.
